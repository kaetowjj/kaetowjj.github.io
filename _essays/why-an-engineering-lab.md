## A place to think
This is Demo writing, not the site owner's personal story. An engineering lab can make the reasoning behind a decision visible.
## A useful habit
Write down the question before the solution. Return later and record what changed.
## Leaving room
A small note that is revisited can be more useful than a polished statement that is never tested.

<div data-lang="zh" markdown="1">

## 留下思考的空间
这是 Demo 内容，并非站点主人的真实经历。工程笔记可以帮助我们看清决策背后的理由。
## 一个有用的习惯
在方案之前先写下问题。之后回来记录发生了什么变化。
## 保持开放
一篇持续修订的小笔记，可能比从不检验的结论更有价值。
